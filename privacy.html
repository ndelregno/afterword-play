Afterword privacy

Afterword is a free word game, run as a hobby project. Last updated November 2026.

The short version

You can play without signing in. If you do sign in, we keep only what the leaderboard needs. We never sell or share your information, show no ads and use no trackers.

Stored only on your device

Your high scores, game settings and the pairs you add with "That should count" are saved in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

Pairs you suggest

When you tap "That should count", the two words, how you said they're written and the game mode are sent to our review inbox, along with a random ID created by your browser so we can count how many different players asked for a pair. Only a scrambled (hashed) form of that ID is stored. Your IP address is used to prevent flooding and is never stored; we keep only a scrambled form of it combined with the date, which changes every day and can't be turned back into your address, so that one connection can't pose as many players. Reviewed pairs may be added to the game's word list for everyone.

Words the game doesn't know

When the game refuses a pair as "not in the word list", it sends the two words and the game mode to our server, with the same random browser ID, so we can spot phrases many players expect that the list is missing. Nothing else about you or your game is sent. Only the hashed ID and the same daily scrambled form of your connection are stored, each pair is counted once per browser, and pairs are deleted once reviewed or after about six months.

Feedback you send

When you use Send feedback, your message, the kind of message, the game version and, if you typed one, your name or email are sent to our server, plus your browser type for problem reports. The same daily scrambled form of your connection is kept to limit how many messages one connection can send. Messages are read by the site owner and deleted from the server once they've been passed on.

Signing in and the leaderboard

If you choose to sign in so your scores appear on the leaderboard, we store:

  • the username you pick, which is shown publicly on the leaderboard;
  • your email address, either the one you type or the one your Google account provides, kept private and never shown on the leaderboard;
  • if you use Google sign-in, your Google account's ID, so we can recognize you next time;
  • your game results: scores, number of links or tries, and dates.

We don't receive or store passwords. Google sign-in only shares your basic profile (name, email and account ID) with us. A sign-in token kept in your browser keeps you signed in on that device until you sign out.

Where it's kept

Suggestions and leaderboard data are stored with Cloudflare, which hosts our small server. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Removing your data

To have your account and scores deleted, or for any question about this page, contact the site owner through github.com/ndelregno.
