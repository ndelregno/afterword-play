About Afterword

A word-chain game, still in testing.

How it started

The game

Each word you add has to follow the one before it to make a compound word or a common two-word phrase: dog → house → party makes doghouse and house party. Endless asks how long a chain you can build; the daily Ladder asks you to link a start word to an end word in a set number of steps.

The word list

The list holds over 100,000 pairs, gathered from dictionaries, crossword word lists and books, then checked by hand and with AI helpers. It's still growing: when you tap That should count, the pair is reviewed every morning and, if it's a real phrase, added for everyone. Sources and licenses are on the privacy & credits page.
